Literature notes actively being worked on, before promotion to `03_KNOWLEDGE/literature/`. Notes are tracked by git; PDFs are ignored globally (`*.pdf`), so they never enter the vault.

Transit zone only, not a reading archive. The PDF never enters the vault — Zotero remains the sole PDF repository. Once the note is reviewed and ready to link into the knowledge network, move it to `03_KNOWLEDGE/literature/`.
