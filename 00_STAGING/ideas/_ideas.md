Pre-project hypotheses and scientific sparks — the "composting" zone for ideas, driven by `check_idea_decay.py`. Files named `YYYY-MM-DD_short-title.md` with `type: idea`, `status: raw|developing|→ project`, and a `decay` field controlling alert frequency.

`decay` values: `never` (can sleep 12 months without alert), `soft` (alert after 180 days), `aggressive` (alert after 30 days). Don't put ideas here if they're already tied to a known active project — use that project's own writing/fragments folder instead.
