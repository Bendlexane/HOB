The month-by-month planning cycle — a "plan vs. review" loop that turns KPIs into decisions. One sub-folder per month, `YYYY-MM/`, meant to hold a `_plan.md` (drafted from the previous month's KPIs, then reviewed by you) and a `_review.md` (end-of-month retrospective).

*Not automated in this beta — no generation script ships yet, write yours as a template if you want one.*
