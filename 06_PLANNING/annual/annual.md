The yearly synthesis — the highest-altitude view of research activity. A `YYYY_annual-report.md` aggregates the whole year's KPIs, compares them against the previous year, and can add an LLM narrative draft. Designed to be reused for CV, evaluations, and grant applications.

*Not automated in this beta — no report-generation script ships yet, write yours as a template if you want one.*
