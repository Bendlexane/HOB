Travel, conferences, and fieldwork missions, organized in two subfolders:

| Subfolder | Contents | Template |
|---|---|---|
| `conferences/` | Conferences, workshops, symposia | 🎯 New Conference (`mission.md`) |
| `fieldwork/` | Fieldwork, sampling activities | 🥾 Fieldwork (`fieldwork.md`) |

Each mission folder contains `administration/` (authorizations) and `expenses/` (receipts, invoices, tickets), plus type-specific content.
